## Scheduled Downtime

Please be advised that **Data Node 02** will be taken offline starting this **Friday at 22:00 ICT**. 

We are upgrading the internal NVMe storage arrays and applying new thermal compounds to the primary compute units. All active training jobs running on Node 02 must be paused and checkpointed before the cutoff time. Jobs left running will be terminated automatically.

**Estimated Uptime:** Sunday, 08:00 ICT.
