# ACOMPA Success

Our research team recently returned from the ACOMPA in Vietnam. We presented our latest paper on utilizing synthetic data augmentation to reduce hallucination rates in Retrieval-Augmented Generation architectures.

The presentation was met with excellent feedback, and we look forward to integrating these imbalanced learning frameworks into our upcoming edge hardware deployments.

## Future Framework Integration

### Hardware Optimization & Edge Deployment

Following the structural confirmation of our RAG architectures at ACOMPA, our immediate next step involves optimizing these systems for localized environments. Transitioning these complex models from cloud-based computational clusters to constrained edge hardware requires aggressive quantization and pruning strategies. This process ensures high-fidelity performance without sacrificing the latency baselines necessary for real-time execution.

## Next Implementation Phases

### Porting Imbalanced Learning to Edge Devices

The primary bottleneck in deployment remains handling highly skewed data distributions directly on the edge node. By deploying the imbalanced learning frameworks validated during our recent benchmarks, the system can dynamically adjust its internal scoring mechanisms during inference. This ongoing development will directly stabilize the model’s performance on low-power architectures, ensuring consistent error mitigation across irregular edge workloads.
